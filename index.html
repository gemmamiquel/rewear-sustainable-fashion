01 / The manifesto

New style.
Same clothes.

Fashion doesn't always need a new purchase. Sometimes it needs a new perspective.

01 — THINK

Pause.

Before buying something new, take a moment. Do you really need it? Could something you already own work instead?

02 — RE-MIX

Re-style.

Rediscover your wardrobe. Combine familiar pieces in unexpected ways and make old favourites feel new.

03 — REPEAT

Repeat.

Wearing something again is not boring. It is a small, everyday way to make more conscious choices.

02 / The interactive edit

Your style
studio.

A digital space to rediscover the clothes you already have.

Build your digital closet by describing your clothes. RE:WEAR will create a visual interpretation of each piece, then use your wardrobe to imagine new outfits.

01 / Digital closet 0 pieces

No photo uploads. Just describe your garment. AI will create an illustrative image of it. The image and item are saved in this browser.

02 / Style remix AI styling experiment

Turn your own wardrobe into new outfit ideas. Choose at least two pieces and let AI combine them into a look.

For the best results, add a mix of tops, bottoms, dresses, shoes and accessories. Generated outfits are creative interpretations, not exact product replicas.

03 / Before you buy

The impact
check.

A small pause can make a big difference. Think before adding something to your wardrobe.

1. How often do you think you would wear this item?

2. Have you checked whether you already own something similar?

3. Have you considered buying it second-hand?
