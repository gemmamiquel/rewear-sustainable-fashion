01 / The manifesto

New style.
Same clothes.

Fashion doesn't always need a new purchase. Sometimes it needs a new perspective.

01 — THINK

Pause.

Before buying something new, take a moment. Do you really need it? Could something you already own work instead?

02 — RE-MIX

Re-style.

Rediscover your wardrobe. Combine familiar pieces in unexpected ways and make old favourites feel new.

03 — REPEAT

Repeat.

Wearing something again is not boring. It is a small, everyday way to make more conscious choices.

02 / The interactive edit

Your style
studio.

A digital space to rediscover the clothes you already have.

Build your digital closet by describing your clothes. RE:WEAR will create a simple visual illustration of each piece, then arrange your wardrobe into new outfit combinations.

01 / Digital closet0 pieces

No photo uploads and no paid AI service. Add a description and RE:WEAR will create a simple garment illustration. Your closet is saved in this browser.

02 / Style remixWardrobe combinations

Explore combinations made from the clothes in your digital closet. Select at least two pieces and RE:WEAR will arrange them into outfit ideas.

Style Remix creates visual outfit boards from your saved pieces. It does not generate a photograph of a person wearing the clothes.

03 / Before you buy

The impact
check.

A small pause can make a big difference. Think before adding something to your wardrobe.

1. How often do you think you would wear this item?

2. Have you checked whether you already own something similar?

3. Have you considered buying it second-hand?
